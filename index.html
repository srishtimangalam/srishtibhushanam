About Us

Param Pujya Aryika Ratna 105 Shri Sristhi Bhushan Mataji was born in Mungaoli, a sacred town in Madhya Pradesh. The entire town was blessed by the birth of this divine soul. She filled the home of her virtuous mother Smt. Padma and ideal householder father Shri Kapoorchand with divine radiance.
